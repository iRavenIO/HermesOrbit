# Investor Narrative

## 1. One-line pitch

HermesOrbit is a native iOS control plane for Hermes Agent that lets users monitor, manage, and intervene in real AI agent workflows from anywhere.

## 2. The problem

AI agents are becoming more useful, more autonomous, and more embedded in real work.
But the control surfaces are still too desktop-bound.

Today, users can run Hermes through CLI, messaging, and the web dashboard. That works well at a desk. It works less well when the user is:
- traveling
- in meetings
- away from the laptop
- watching long-running jobs
- waiting for an approval or intervention moment

The result is a trust gap.
The agent may be running, but the user does not feel fully in control.

## 3. The product thesis

HermesOrbit closes that trust gap.

It is not another chatbot.
It is a **mobile operator console** for people whose AI agents already matter.

The app should let a user:
- check health and status fast
- continue or redirect work from mobile
- manage jobs and automation
- handle approvals and interruptions
- administer important Hermes settings safely
- move from self-hosted Hermes today to managed-hosted Hermes later

## 4. Why this can win

### 4.1 The category is real
The market has already validated:
- AI agents as a budget category
- mobile AI apps as a behavior category
- self-hosted/private AI as a trust category
- remote agent supervision as a workflow category

### 4.2 The wedge is sharp
HermesOrbit does not need to beat broad consumer AI apps at everything.
It only needs to dominate one high-value workflow:

> **operator-grade mobile control of meaningful agent systems**

That is a better wedge because it is:
- more urgent
- more defensible
- more premium
- more operationally sticky

### 4.3 The self-hosted-to-hosted arc is commercially strong
The app can start with:
- self-hosted technical users
- founders
- infra-minded operators

Then expand into:
- hosted individual plans
- hosted pro plans
- team and enterprise control layers

That means the same product can serve as both:
- a high-value self-hosted tool
- the customer control surface for a future managed business

## 5. Why mobile matters strategically

If AI agents work asynchronously, the user needs asynchronous control.
That is the central product logic.

A phone is the natural device for:
- alerts
- approvals
- status checks
- stop/go decisions
- fast interventions
- command redirection

If the product does not own those moments, the workflow remains incomplete.

## 6. The moat

HermesOrbit’s moat is not “we have an LLM in an app.”
It is the combination of:
- Hermes-native workflow parity
- mobile-native trust and control design
- multi-instance operator UX
- self-hosted credibility
- future hosted expansion path

That moat strengthens as:
- more operator workflows are encoded into the product
- mobile trust patterns improve
- hosted account infrastructure is added
- more switching cost accumulates through environments, automation, and habits

## 7. Business model direction

Recommended commercial sequence:

### Stage 1
Self-hosted app with:
- free entry or low-friction adoption
- Pro tier for advanced notification routing, richer approval ergonomics, premium operator views, widgets, shortcuts, and multi-instance convenience while baseline approval/failure/completion push remains core

Commercial rule: launch scope still follows `V1_SCOPE_DECISIONS.md`; paid tiers expand convenience, routing, limits, and premium operator workflows rather than redefining what the core launch product is.

### Stage 2
Hosted Hermes plans with the same mobile app as the control center.

### Stage 3
Team and enterprise layers:
- shared environments
- RBAC
- audit exports
- support and compliance features

## 8. Risks investors will ask about

### “Why not just use the web dashboard?”
Because native mobile workflows are meaningfully better for alerts, trust, permissions, deeplinks, and intervention speed.

### “Is this too niche?”
The initial wedge is intentionally focused. The users are higher intent and more likely to pay than general AI chat users.

### “Can Apple reject this?”
Yes, if the app is vague, deceptive, unsafe, or commercially messy. That is why App Review readiness, branding independence, privacy disclosures, and review-safe demo flows are built into the documentation from the start.

### “What if a bigger AI app copies this?”
Large general AI apps can copy surface features, but they are less likely to build a deeply Hermes-native operator console with self-hosted credibility and multi-environment administrative depth.

## 9. What success looks like

The product wins if users say:
- “I trust my agents more because I can see and control them from my phone.”
- “I do not need to run back to my laptop for every approval or failure.”
- “This feels like the real control center, not a companion toy.”

## 10. Investor conclusion

HermesOrbit is compelling because it is not trying to be a generic AI product.
It is trying to become the mobile control plane for a category that is already emerging:

**people who run AI agents for real work and need trustworthy control away from the desktop.**

That is a credible product wedge, a credible self-hosted entry point, and a credible foundation for a future hosted business.
